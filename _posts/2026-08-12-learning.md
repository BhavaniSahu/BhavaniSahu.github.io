AI is a huge field with many subfields. Here are some of the main ones:

- **Machine Learning (ML):** Teaching computers to learn from data.
- **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Helping machines understand and generate human language.
- **Computer Vision:** Enabling computers to interpret and understand images and videos.
- **Robotics:** Building machines that can move and interact with the physical world.
- **Expert Systems:** Designing systems that mimic human decision-making.
- **Speech Recognition:** Understanding and processing spoken language.
- **Planning and Scheduling:** Getting machines to plan actions to achieve goals.
- **Reinforcement Learning:** Training agents to make decisions through trial and error.

**ML (Machine Learning):** This is the broader field of teaching computers to learn from data and make predictions or decisions without explicit instructions. ML includes many types of models—some for images, numbers, or language.

**Model:** In machine learning, a "model" is any system trained to make predictions or decisions from data. Models can do all sorts of things—recognize images, predict numbers, translate languages, etc.

**LLM (Large Language Model):** This is a specific kind of machine learning model trained on lots of text data to understand and generate human language (like me!). GPT-4, for example, is an LLM.

An LLM is a kind of model, but not all models are LLMs!

All LLMs are ML, but not all ML models are LLMs!

So, ML is the field, a model is a tool built using ML, and an LLM is a specific kind of model focused on language!

How is Planning and Scheduling different from automation or orchestration

- **Planning and Scheduling:** This is an AI subfield focused on figuring out the best sequence of actions to reach a specific goal, often under constraints (like time, resources, or dependencies). It’s about decision-making—what to do, and when.

- **Automation:** This is using technology to perform tasks without human intervention. Automation can use planning and scheduling, but often just follows predefined rules or scripts.

- **Orchestration:** This is managing and coordinating multiple automated tasks or processes, often across different systems, to achieve a larger objective. Orchestration decides how tasks fit together, but doesn’t always “plan” in the smart, adaptive way AI does.

In short:  
- **Planning and Scheduling** = Smart decision-making about actions and timing  
- **Automation** = Doing tasks automatically  
- **Orchestration** = Coordinating many automated tasks
